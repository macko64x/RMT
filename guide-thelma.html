You're at 11,200 feet

Altitude is the one thing that catches people out up here. Take it seriously and you'll feel great.

  • Drink far more water than feels necessary, go easy on alcohol the first night, and don't be surprised if you sleep poorly or get a headache day one.
  • Know the signs of altitude sickness — worsening headache, nausea, dizziness, shortness of breath at rest. The only sure fix is to go down. If symptoms are severe or include confusion or trouble breathing, descend immediately and call 911.
  • Ascending gradually helps — a night in Ouray, Silverton or Durango before you come up makes a real difference.
